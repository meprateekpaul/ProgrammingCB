Pedagogical Philosophy

The Continuous Biological Thread

Rather than isolated, disposable toy problems, students follow four canonical biological entities across the entire semester—witnessing how representation evolves from raw text strings to object hierarchies, interactive 3D structures, and genomic data frames.

Human TP53

Tumor Suppressor

Guardian of the genome. Analyzed through mRNA translation (M01), GenBank feature annotation (M02), mutation hot-spot modeling (M03), PDB 1TUP 3D structure contact maps (M05B), and differential expression volcano plots (M04/M06).

Human INS

Peptide Hormone

Preproprotein cleavage mechanics. Dissected via signal peptides, B-chain, C-peptide, and A-chain proteolytic maturation. Tracks UniProt REST record harvesting (M05B) and post-translational disulfide bond verification.

Human HBB

Oxygen Transport

Hemoglobin subunit beta. Ground zero for variant modeling: students compute the clinical Glu6Val (HbS sickle cell) missense mutation, string hamming distance, and restriction enzyme digest site alteration.

E. coli lacZ

Bacterial Operon

The archetypal prokaryotic gene regulation circuit. Students extract the polycistronic lac operon from complete bacterial genomes, parse regulatory operator regions, and translate the 1,024-amino-acid β-galactosidase polypeptide.
